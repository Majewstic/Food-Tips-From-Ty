Breakfast Burrito

Description:

This recipe is hearty and delicious, while also being light on the stomach and healthy. Packed with protein it features ground turkey, egg, cottage cheese, spinach, tater tots for texture and fluff, and finally pinto and refried beans. This combo turns a mundane breakfast into a meal for a warrior.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground turkey
  • 6 large tortillas
  • 18 eggs
  • 1 can refried beans & pinto beans each
  • 1 bag tater tots
  • roughly 3 cups spinach (measure with your heart on this one)
  • 1 pound low fat cottage cheese
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp onion & garlic powder each
  • 1/2 tbsp cumin
  • 2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp red chili pepper
  • pinch of oregano

Instructions:

  1. Step one: Prep

    Pre-heat the oven for tater tots. Measure out all the seasoning into a small bowl. Crack all eggs into a bowl and whisk them. Set turkey, spinach, cottage cheese, and beans to the side.

  2. Step 2: Turkey

    Put the turkey on the stove set to medium high. Add all the seasoning and mix. Once that has started looking halfway cooked, add cottage cheese and spinach to the mix. Let that simmer on the stove and stir occationally.

  3. Step 3: beans

    Pour both cans of beans into the same pot, (optional) squeeze a 2 or 3 lemon slices into it, then stir. Heat up slightly and keep warm for mixing.

  4. Step 4: Set turkey aside

    After the moisture in the turkey mix has reduced to the point where it has thickened, set aside the mixture in a large bowl or container.

  5. Step 5: Eggs

    After taking the turkey off the stove, keep the heat at the same temp (medium high) and pour the eggs into the pot. Salt them, and pepper if you wish. Stir constantly until the eggs have a creamy consistently, then put them in the same bowl with the turkey mixture.

  6. Step 6: The mixture

    Mix beans into the bowl that you have placed the turkey and eggs. Mix for an even spread of ingredients across burrito.

  7. Step 7: The wrap

    Now you are going to get your tortillas (roughly 6 to 7 for this recipe) and heat them on the stove. If possible, spray them slightly with water to steam them slightly, this adds more flexibility to the tortilla. After heating them on both sides, place them on a surface for wrapping. You will want to add roughly 1 to 1 1/4th cup of the mixture onto each burrito. If you have the space I would recommend prepping them all at once, to make sure you have an even spread of mixture. Finally, wrap the burrito and set aside.

  8. Step 8a: Storage

    If you are planning on storing these for later consumption, gather some parchment paper and a small bowl of olive oil, as well as a basting brush. Take the burritos and brush a small amount of olive oil on two sides, then wrap in parchment paper. Store in the freezer within a freezer bag for best long term storage. Reheat using either microwave, stove, or airfrier.

  9. Step 8b: Eating right now

    If you plan to eat your burritos right then, grab another clean pan and place on the stove at high heat. Spread a small amount of olive oil onto pan, then place burrito fold side down to sear. Sear for roughly 30 seconds, or until burrito is golden brown. Flip over to opposite side and repeat. After, place on a plate and enjoy your meal, warrior ;).
