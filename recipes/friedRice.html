Fried Rice!

This meal is all about stretching cheap food into a large meal. Pricing around $30 (depending on locale) this meal is PACKED full of yummy healthy ingredients. Enough to fill you, whlie being relatively cheap to make and healthy.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pound chicken
  • 8oz/225g shiitake mushrooms
  • 1/2 head cabbage
  • 4oz/110g carrots
  • 6 eggs
  • (at least) 1 cup/500g rice
  • 1c/500g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp garlic & onion powder
  • 112.5g oyster sauce
  • 75g low sodium soy sauce
  • 22.5g sesame oil
  • Sriracha mayo

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Prep

    Make sure to cut your chicken, dice your cabbage, carrots, and mushrooms, pre-cook your rice, and heat up your peas. Also prep stir fry sauce (oyster sauce + soy sauce + sesame oil) in a small dish for later.

  2. Step 2: Mushrooms

    Grab a big bowl and set it aside. Then grab a large pot and place it on the stove at medium-high heat. Add in oil, mushrooms, and salt. Make sure to go light on the salt, because we will add salt at each step. Cooked until browned and set aside in bowl.

  3. Step 3: C & C

    Next put your carrots and cabbage in the pot, salt, and place lid over for roughly 5 minutes. Afterwards, dump into same bowl as mushrooms.

  4. Step 4: Egg

    Add oil, salt, and eggs to pan. Scramble them and add to bowl.

  5. Step 5: The Clucker

    Add chicken to pot, season well with garlic and onion powder. Roughly 1 tbsp but measure this with your heart. After the meat is cooked thoroughly, keep in pot for step 6.

  6. Step 6: Ricer

    Add the rice, peas, and stir fry sauce to the pot. Stir for for 3 minutes to reduce sauce and mix well.

  7. Step 7: Wombo Combo

    Add in the ingredients within the bowl. Be careful as your pot will be very full. Mix carefully to stir all ingredients together to create even blend.

  8. Step 8: Sloppy Toppy

    Serve your Fried Rice into a bowl, grab the sriracha mayo and drizzle on top to your liking. Enjoy your Fried Rice!!
